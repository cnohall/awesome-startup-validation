## AI Validators
- [IdeaHunter](https://ideahunter.today) — AI research for demand-backed app and micro-SaaS ideas. Freemium.
- [IdeaRoast](https://idearoast.dev) — Brutal AI roast of your startup idea using live market data. Pay-per-use.
- [IdeaProof](https://ideaproof.com) — Market analysis, TAM/SAM/SOM, competitor maps. Credit-based.
- [ValidatorAI](https://validator.ai) — Startup score, roadmap, AI mentorship. Freemium.
- [TestYourIdea](https://testyouridea.com) — 50+ criteria scan across PH, Crunchbase, Reddit. Per-report pricing.

## Market Research
- [SparkToro](https://sparktoro.com) — Audience research: where your customers hang out online.
- [Similarweb](https://similarweb.com) — Competitor traffic & market sizing.
- [Wynter](https://wynter.com) — B2B messaging research with real buyers.

## Trend Discovery
- [Google Trends](https://trends.google.com) — Free search trend data.
- [Exploding Topics](https://explodingtopics.com) — Early-stage trend detection.
- [Product Hunt](https://producthunt.com) — See what's launching, what's getting traction.

## Demand Testing
- [BetaList](https://betalist.com) — Launch early, collect signups before building.
- [Waiting List](https://waitinglist.com) — Simple waitlist pages for pre-launch validation.
- [Carrd](https://carrd.co) — Fast landing pages to test demand.

## Frameworks & Canvases
- [Lean Canvas](https://leanstack.com) — One-page business model for early-stage startups.
- [Strategyzer](https://strategyzer.com) — Business Model Canvas + Value Proposition Canvas.
- [Y Combinator Startup School](https://startupschool.org) — Free curriculum + idea validation resources.
